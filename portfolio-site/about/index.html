WHY iMARKED EXISTS

A little more room
for good work.

iMarked is an open directory that helps independent creatives share what they do and helps curious people find them.

01 / THE IDEA

Make discovery easier

Creative work grows through conversation. This directory gives people a simple place to introduce their practice, explore different disciplines, and find potential collaborators.

02 / THE APPROACH

Keep it human

Profiles put each person and their work first. There are no rankings to chase; search and discipline filters help visitors find what they came for.

03 / YOUR PROFILE

Start with the essentials

Add your name, focus, location, a short introduction, and a link to your work. Profiles created here are saved in your current browser.
